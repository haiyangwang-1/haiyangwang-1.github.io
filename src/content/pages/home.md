I am an Applied Mathematics Ph.D. researcher at **Yale University**, advised by Yihong Wu and Ronald Coifman. My work connects mathematical structure with practical computation across machine learning, information theory, and numerical analysis.

## Research directions

### Machine learning theory

I study the statistical foundations of gradient-boosted trees, including convergence, principled parameter choices, and learning with missing covariates.

### Information theory

I investigate amplitude-constrained Gaussian channels through approximation theory. This perspective produced a new super-linear lower bound on the support size of capacity-achieving input distributions.

### Scientific computing

I develop fast numerical solvers for PDEs and integral equations. At the Flatiron Institute, I helped build a high-order scattering method for Stokes flow that achieved a **100× speedup** in large channel geometries.
