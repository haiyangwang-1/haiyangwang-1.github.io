I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Yale University. I work with **Yihong Wu** and **Ronald Coifman** on problems where probability, approximation, learning, and computation meet.

My current research has two main theoretical directions. The first develops foundations for XGBoost and related tree-based learning methods, with particular attention to convergence and missing covariates. The second studies optimal distributions for amplitude-constrained Gaussian channels using tools from approximation theory.

Before Yale, I was a Research Analyst at the Flatiron Institute's Center for Computational Mathematics, where I worked with Leslie Greengard and Manas Rachh on fast solvers for Stokes, Helmholtz, and Lippmann–Schwinger equations. I also contributed to the [chunkIE](https://github.com/fastalgorithms/chunkie) integral-equation toolbox.

## Interests
