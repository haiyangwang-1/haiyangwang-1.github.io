This work converts an optimal-distribution problem into best approximation of a target law on a compact domain. The resulting framework proves an $\Omega(A\sqrt{\log A})$ lower bound on the support size of the capacity-achieving input distribution, improving the previous linear bound and ruling out a longstanding linear-scaling conjecture.

**Award:** 2026 IEEE Jack Keil Wolf ISIT Student Paper Award.
